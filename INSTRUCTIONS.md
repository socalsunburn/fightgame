# Fight Game — How to Play

## Controls

### Player 1 — Blue
| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Move left / right | A / D |
| Jump | W |
| Double jump | W again (in air) |
| Fast fall | S (while falling) |
| Jab / Charged jab | F (tap = weak, hold + release = charged) |
| Up tilt | W + F (on ground) |
| Down tilt | S + F (on ground) |
| Air attack | F (while airborne) |
| Special | G |
| Shield | H |

### Player 2 — Red
| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Move left / right | ← / → |
| Jump | ↑ |
| Double jump | ↑ again (in air) |
| Fast fall | ↓ (while falling) |
| Jab / Charged jab | L (tap = weak, hold + release = charged) |
| Up tilt | ↑ + L (on ground) |
| Down tilt | ↓ + L (on ground) |
| Air attack | L (while airborne) |
| Special | K |
| Shield | J |

> Gamepads supported — plug in before starting. A = jump, X = attack, B = special, LB = shield.

---

## How to Win

- Each player starts with **3 stocks** (lives shown as coloured squares under the damage %)
- Hitting your opponent increases their **damage %** — higher % means hits send them further
- You score a **KO** by knocking the opponent past the blast zone (far off any edge)
- The last player with stocks remaining wins

---

## Charged Jab

Hold your attack button on the ground (no directional input). Your character will **grow and glow** as the charge builds over 1 second. Release to fire — a fully charged hit does 2.5× damage and 3× base knockback compared to a tap. Jump cancels the charge.

## Characters

### Brawler — Heavy / Slow
The foundation character. Big hitboxes, strong knockback, but slow to start moves.
- **Special:** Wide power punch. Long startup but launches hard.
- **Tip:** Patient ground game. Punish whiffs with charged jab.

### Swift — Light / Fast
Fastest normal character. Quick attacks, but lighter so easier to KO.
- **Special:** Upward burst. Launches opponents straight up — good for combos.
- **Tip:** Use air attacks to chase opponents off stage.

### Phantom — Glass Cannon
Extremely fast and hits surprisingly hard. Flies off-stage at low damage though.
- **Special:** Huge burst with very high knockback scaling. Risky to use, devastating on hit.
- **Tip:** Phantom's charged jab is the strongest in the game — use it to win neutral.

### Volt — Zoner
Average in most stats, but special fires a **projectile** that travels across the stage.
- **Special:** Electric bolt. Forces the opponent to move or take damage.
- **Tip:** Fire a bolt then approach — the opponent has to deal with both threats at once.

### Crusher — Grappler
Heaviest, slowest character. Hard to KO, but struggles to land hits.
- **Special:** Grab attack — extremely close range, but launches opponents hard at **any damage %**. Low scaling means it's a threat even at 0%.
- **Tip:** Shield opponents into a corner, then grab. Patience wins.

### Mirror — Counter
Balanced stats. Special puts the character in a **counter stance** (flashing white).
- **Special:** If hit while flashing, Mirror absorbs the hit and **reflects it back at 2× knockback**. Expires after ~0.5 seconds if nothing connects.
- **Tip:** Use counter to punish predictable attacks. Don't spam it — a missed counter leaves you wide open.
